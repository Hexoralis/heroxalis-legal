# Heroxalis — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

Heroxalis ("we", "our", or "us") develops and operates Aina, an AI-powered Discord bot that provides conversational interactions and assistance.

This Privacy Policy explains how Aina may collect, use, process, and retain information when you interact with the bot.

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Aina may process the following information:

- **Discord Information:** Discord user IDs, usernames, server IDs, and channel IDs necessary for bot functionality.
- **Messages and Commands:** Messages, commands, and prompts you send to Aina so the bot can understand and respond to your requests.
- **Conversation Data:** Conversation history or preferences, if these features are enabled.
- **Technical Information:** Error reports, diagnostic logs, and interaction records needed to maintain and troubleshoot the service.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to operate and improve Aina.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Aina may be used to:

- Respond to messages and commands.
- Generate AI-powered responses.
- Maintain conversation context when supported.
- Diagnose errors and improve reliability.
- Prevent abuse and protect the service.

We do not intend to use personal information for purposes unrelated to operating the project without an appropriate basis.

## 3. AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Aina may rely on external AI providers or hosting services to operate.

When external providers are used, information necessary to process your requests may be transmitted to those services. Their handling of information may be governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Before enabling these integrations, we will identify the relevant providers and review their data-handling practices.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

The information Aina processes may be temporarily held in memory, recorded in logs, or stored in a database, depending on the features and infrastructure in use.

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legitimate operational, security, and legal requirements.

Actual retention periods depend on the systems and features enabled. We will not promise automatic deletion or zero data retention unless those practices are implemented and verified.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or AI service providers when necessary to operate Aina. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or our rights.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Aina. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Users should avoid sending passwords, authentication tokens, financial details, or other highly sensitive information to the bot.

## 7. Your Choices and Requests

You may stop interacting with Aina at any time.

If you would like to ask about personal information associated with your use of Aina, or request access to or deletion of eligible stored information, contact us through our official support channel.

Requests will be reviewed and handled where reasonably possible and as required by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Aina is not intended to encourage children to share personal or sensitive information. Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws when using the service.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Aina's features, infrastructure, or data practices change. The latest version will be published in this repository, with the revision date updated accordingly.

## 10. Contact

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the Heroxalis project through its official support channel.
