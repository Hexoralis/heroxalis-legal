Privacy Policy

Aina · Heroxalis / Aina Project

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Heroxalis ("we", "our", or "us") develops and operates Aina, an AI-powered Discord bot designed for conversational interactions and assistance. This policy explains how information may be processed when you use Aina.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Aina may process:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames, server IDs, and channel IDs needed for operation.
  • Messages, commands, and prompts you send to Aina.
  • Conversation history or preferences, if these features are enabled.
  • Technical logs, error reports, and diagnostic information used to maintain the service.

We aim to process only information reasonably necessary to operate and maintain Aina.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to requests, generate AI responses, maintain conversation context, troubleshoot errors, improve reliability, and protect the service from abuse.

3. AI and Third-Party Services

Aina may use external AI providers, hosting platforms, or other infrastructure services. Information necessary to fulfill your request may be transmitted to those providers and handled under their respective policies.

The providers and data practices actually used by Aina depend on its current configuration. We will update this policy as those practices change.

4. Storage and Retention

Depending on the features and infrastructure enabled, information may be temporarily held in memory or recorded in logs or databases. Retention periods depend on the systems in use and operational, security, or legal requirements.

We do not promise zero retention or automatic deletion unless those practices are implemented and verified.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate Aina, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or our rights.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Aina. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending passwords, authentication tokens, financial details, or other highly sensitive information to the bot.

7. Your Choices and Data Requests

You can stop using Aina at any time. For questions about your information or requests to access or delete eligible stored data, contact the project through its official support channel.

Requests will be reviewed and handled where reasonably possible and as required by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws. Aina is not intended to encourage children to share personal or sensitive information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Aina's features, infrastructure, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this website with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Heroxalis through its official support channel.
